# Company and Site

Orchestrate is an agent-native holding company that builds and operates products
through human judgment, agent execution, shared infrastructure, and disciplined
portfolio allocation. The full thesis and operating model live in the Orchestrate
Linear initiative document.

This repository renders the public partner-facing explanation. Its audience includes
banking, institutional, and potential operating partners. The page should be factual,
specific, and credible without implying that agents replace human direction, risk
judgment, review, or allocation.

The legal repository identity is currently Orchestrate Inc.; the public domain and
contact use Orchestrate Holdings. The site-readiness issue must confirm the intended
legal/public-brand hierarchy before changing those surfaces.
